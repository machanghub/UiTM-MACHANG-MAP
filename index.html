Choose a destination, then use your current location or tap “Choose start point on map”. You can also tap any place on the map to set it as the destination.

Location pins below are starter examples and must be checked against the official campus map before public launch. Walking/driving routes depend on available mapped paths and the routing service; verify all routes on campus.
